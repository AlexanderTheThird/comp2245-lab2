The Principles of Studying

October 3th, 2026 by Joshua

Retaining a mass of information is something I struggle with all of the time. In response to it, I began trying to stick to strict study times this semester. While I was looking up optimal times, however, I found an interesting insight from a stranger online; "I try to study something for as long as it takes me to grasp it just once."

After reading and sitting with it for a few weeks now, I can say for certain that the approach has much better aided my studies. Since, instead of studying for an hour, barely recalling a thing, and then moving on to another topic, I've started that process of cementing the information in my mind in the first session, however long it happened to be. I generally still take an hour, to be clear, but my focus has definitely been altered.
